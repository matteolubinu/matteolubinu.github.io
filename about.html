About

Info

Passionate about technology since I was a kid, I had no doubts when it was time to decide what to do with my future: solve problems through technology. My path started as a front-end developer, bringing a creative approach to solving users' problems, with the goal of creating products and experiences that people love to use.

What was missing from my experience, though, was a fundamental element: knowledge of the user. Now, through a process of research and analysis, I can better understand their needs, goals and problems. Based on that, I'm able to create a product or service with a greater impact and a practical outlook. I also consider myself a collaborative, precise and curious person, with a sharp attention to detail, who embraces experimentation and isn't afraid of getting things wrong.
